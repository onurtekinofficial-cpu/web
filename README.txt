PORTFOLIO WEBSITE — FILES
  index.html  -> your public website (homepage)
  data.js     -> all your content (videos, text, settings)
  admin.html  -> editor. Keep it on your computer (recommended); don't rely on it reading data.js.

DEPLOY (GitHub Pages)
  1. Upload index.html and data.js to the TOP LEVEL of your repo (name it USERNAME.github.io).
  2. Settings -> Pages -> Deploy from a branch -> main -> / (root) -> Save.
  3. Open https://USERNAME.github.io

EDIT CONTENT
  1. Open admin.html in the same browser every time.
  2. Change content, then click "Publish (Download data.js)".
  3. Upload the new data.js to GitHub (replace the old one). Hard-refresh the site.

CONTACT FORM
  Admin -> SEO & Settings: enter your Gmail (and optional Formspree URL), Save, Publish, upload data.js.

NOTES
  - Replace the sample (demo) video and image URLs with your own full https:// links.
  - Videos can be direct .mp4 links or YouTube links.
